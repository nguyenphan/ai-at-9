Privacy Policy AI@9

Last updated: November 20, 2026

AI@9 ("we", "us") is a daily AI news video channel available on TikTok and at this website. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information we collect

We do not run accounts, comment systems, analytics trackers, or mailing lists on this website. We do not collect, store, or process personal information from visitors to this site.

If you email us at a1.at.9@outlook.com, we receive the information you send (your email address and message) and use it only to respond to you.

TikTok

When you interact with AI@9 on TikTok (views, likes, comments, follows), that activity is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy. We see only the aggregate statistics and public interactions TikTok makes available to creators.

If you choose to log in with TikTok through any AI@9 application, we request only the basic profile information needed to provide that feature. We do not sell or share it with third parties.

Third-party services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted on our TikTok channel.
